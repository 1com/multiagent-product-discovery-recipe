Interviewer: What's the most frustrating part of all that?

Interviewer: Tell me about the last time this went wrong.
Participant: Not really, I just eat something else if I have nothing.

Interviewer: What tools or apps, if any, do you use for this today?

Interviewer: Who else is involved in deciding what to cook and what to buy?
