## Caveats

- Few runs per condition: differences smaller than the ranges above may be noise.
- The judge is a Gemini model grading Gemini output; it may favour its own style. Trap and misrepresentation verdicts are grounded in verified excerpts (the judge's claim is dropped when its excerpt is not in the brief); criterion scores are not, so check them against the human scores.
- A misrepresentation the judge misses is not counted; faithfulness is an upper bound.
- The data is synthetic and fixed; results describe this case, not discovery work in general.
